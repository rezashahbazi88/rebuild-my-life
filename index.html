How I Got Here

The war and severe economic crisis in Iran had a devastating impact on my work and income. Economic instability, sanctions, inflation, uncertainty, and the disruption of normal business activity have made it extremely difficult for many people to maintain their livelihoods.

My real-estate business eventually became almost impossible to operate, and I had to close my office.

At the same time, I already had significant financial obligations from building my family life. I had been working and paying them down, but when my income disappeared, everything began to fall apart.

In an attempt to protect what little financial security I had left, I made another difficult financial decision and invested most of my remaining savings in Bitcoin. The market subsequently fell, and I lost a significant portion of what remained.

What made the situation even worse was that the period immediately following this financial loss coincided with the escalation of war and economic pressure in Iran.

Since then, the continuing effects of war, sanctions, inflation, and economic instability have pushed my financial situation far beyond what I could manage alone.

I have accumulated serious financial obligations, and some of them have now resulted in legal problems, including enforcement action related to unpaid bank obligations.

The financial pressure has also deeply affected my personal life and marriage. My wife and I are currently living separately, and I am trying to stabilize my life before the consequences become even more serious.
